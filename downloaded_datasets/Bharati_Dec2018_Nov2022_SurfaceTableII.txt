
FORMAT FOR ANTARCTICA DAILY SURFACE TAB2 DATA :
---------------------------------------------
  COL NO.   EXPLANATION
   1 -  5   Five digit No.to designate the station.
   6 -  7   Last two digits of the year.
   8 -  9   Month (01 - January ,..., 12 - December)
  10 - 11   Date

  12 - 15   Maximum Temperature (in 0.1 degree C)
  16 - 19   Minimum Temperature (in 0.1 degree C)  

  20 - 21   Average Wind Speed (in Knots) for 24 hours ending 00 UTC
  22 - 25   Rainfall (in 0.1 mm) in 24 hrs. ending 00 hrs UTC 
  26 - 28   Evaporation (in 0.1 mm) for 24 hours ending at 00 hrs. UTC
  29 - 31   Duration of sunshine (0.1 Hour)

    32      Weather Day with Drizzle
    33      Weather Day with Rain
    34      Weather Day with Snow
    35      Weather Day with Sleet
    36      Weather Day with Hail 
    37      Weather Day with Thunder
    38      Weather Day with Drifting snow
    39      Weather Day with Blowing snow
    40      Weather Day with Fog
    41      Weather Day with Gale

  42 - 43   Type                              |      | 
  44 - 45   Time of Commencement (hour)       |   I  | Description
  46 - 47   Time of Commencement (minutes)    |   I  | of
  48 - 49   Time of Cessation (hour)          |   I  | weather
  50 - 51   Time of Cessation (minutes)       |   I  |

  52 - 53   Type                              |      |
  54 - 55   Time of Commencement (hour)       |  II  | Description
  56 - 57   Time of Commencement (minutes)    |  II  | of
  58 - 59   Time of Cessation (hour)          |  II  | weather
  60 - 61   Time of Cessation (minutes)       |  II  |

  62 - 63   Type                              |      |
  64 - 65   Time of Commencement (hour)       | III  | Description
  66 - 67   Time of Commencement (minutes)    | III  | of
  68 - 69   Time of Cessation (hour)          | III  | weather
  70 - 71   Time of Cessation (minutes)       | III  |
  72 - 77   Blank
  78 - 79   Millenium
    80      Digit '1' is entered to indicate the day's Summary data.
  81 -125   Remarks
